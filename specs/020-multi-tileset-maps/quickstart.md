# Quickstart: Multi Tileset Maps

1. Open a map and paint ground from the default tileset.
2. In the tile palette, switch to another tileset (same display tile size).
3. Paint trees or props on the same layer or on a same/above layer.
4. Toggle the collision overlay: blocking marks come from each cell's tileset.
5. Preview: both images render; the player collides with below-layer solids from each tileset.
